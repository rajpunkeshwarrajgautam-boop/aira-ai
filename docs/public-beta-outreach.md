## Short product description (for reuse)

**AiraAI (beta)** is a web app for **grounded research**: you ask a question, it streams an answer and pulls in **citations from the open web** so you can verify sources. **Standard search** works without an account (with a small guest limit). **Deep Research** and **shareable public report pages** need a **free sign-in**. We’re in **public beta** and want **real feedback** from people running **one genuine research query** (work or learning—not a toy prompt).

---

## Known limitations (say these plainly)

- **Guest mode:** Limited standard searches per day; no saved threads, slash commands, follow-ups, Deep Research, or share links until you sign in.
- **Deep Research / sharing:** Require **sign-in**; sharing builds an **opaque public URL** (don’t treat links as private).
- **Quality:** Answers depend on retrieval and models—**verify critical facts**, especially for decisions with consequences.
- **Beta:** Outages, latency, and rough edges are possible.

## Feedback questions (pick what fits)

1. What **one real query** did you run, and was the **answer + citations** useful end-to-end?
2. Where did the app feel **unclear, slow, or untrustworthy** (UI, sources, or answer quality)?
3. If you **signed in**: did **Deep Research** or **Share result** change how you’d use this versus guest search?
4. What would make you **trust this for work** one notch more—or **not use it again**?
